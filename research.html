Research

My research asks how AI can raise productivity while preserving and fostering human expertise. These are the projects I am currently working on.

Skill erosion and incentives in AI-assisted work

Under review at ACM FAccT 2027 · arXiv:2611.06346

Models how expertise builds through practice and erodes through AI reliance: novices can get trapped at low skill and experts lose skill while AI-assisted accuracy stays high; incentive payments for learning counteract this.

Reliability of the LLMs people rely on

Under review at ICLR 2027 · OpenReview

Disentangling and measuring how sensitive LLM outputs are to prompt formulation.

Scaffolding skill hierarchies in AI-assisted work

Work in progress

A formal model of skill hierarchies, in which higher-order skills rest on foundational tasks that AI now takes over:

  • When does delegating a foundational skill to AI erode the higher-order skills built on it?
  • Which scaffolds (work design, incentives, useful friction) build and preserve skills while also benefiting organizations?
