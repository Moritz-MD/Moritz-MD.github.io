Model

Skill erosion and incentives in AI-assisted work

Under review at ACM FAccT 2027

Expertise builds through practice and erodes through reliance on AI. In our model, novices can get trapped at low skill and experts lose skill while AI-assisted accuracy stays high. Incentive payments for learning counteract this.

arXiv

Scaffolding skill hierarchies in AI-assisted work

Work in progress

Higher-order skills rest on foundational tasks that AI now takes over. I am building a formal model of skill hierarchies to ask:

  • When does delegating a foundational skill to AI erode the higher-order skills built on it?
  • Which scaffolds (work design, incentives, useful friction) build and preserve skills while also benefiting organizations?

LLM-based systems for complex knowledge work

ECIS 2026 · selected for AIS Insights

Together with an SME, we designed an LLM-agent system for public tender analysis. On 34 real tenders it cut initial screening time by 59% at 88.8% retrieval accuracy, and experts shifted from reading documents to orchestrating and verifying the system's output.
