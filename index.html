Hi there, I’m Moritz!

I’m a PhD candidate in Information Systems at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), advised by Gerhard Satzger in the Digital Service Innovation group. I research AI in and for organizations: how organizations can improve productivity with AI while helping people develop and retain expertise. I study when relying on AI leads to skill erosion, how incentives and work design can keep people learning, and how reliable the LLMs are that people depend on.

To answer these questions, I combine formal modeling, LLM evaluation, and systems built and tested with industry partners. I studied Industrial Engineering and Management (B.Sc.) and Information Systems (M.Sc.) at KIT, with exchange semesters in Kaunas and Klagenfurt, and worked on machine learning and technology strategy at TRUUCO, Siemens and Netze BW. Outside work, I train for triathlons, play classical guitar, and spend as much time as I can in the mountains.
