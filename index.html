AI and human expertise at work

How can organizations improve productivity with AI while helping people develop and retain expertise?

I’m a PhD candidate in Information Systems at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), advised by Gerhard Satzger in the Digital Service Innovation group. My work focuses on three questions:

  • Skill erosion and reliance. When does delegating tasks to AI take away the practice people need to develop and retain expertise?
  • Incentives and work design. How can organizations reward independent practice while still using AI where it improves performance?
  • LLM reliability. How much do LLM answers change when people rephrase the same question?

I combine formal modeling, LLM evaluation, and studies of systems built with industry partners. Before my PhD, I studied Industrial Engineering and Management (B.Sc.) and Information Systems (M.Sc.) at KIT, with exchange semesters in Kaunas and Klagenfurt, and worked on machine learning and technology strategy at TRUUCO, Siemens and Netze BW.

Research interests: AI in and for Organizations · Human–AI Decision-Making & Reliance · Human Expertise & Skill Erosion · Incentive & Work Design · LLM Agents in Organizations · Sustainability
