Projects & Teaching

Research projects

ConCIRCLE

Mar 2026 – present · funded by BMFTR

AI for the circular economy in construction, with Ed. Züblin AG, DBV and Concular. With the partners, I identify AI use cases in requirements workshops, then design, implement and evaluate them, focusing on LLM-based information extraction that supports human decisions on building reuse.

NaiS

May 2023 – Jul 2026 · funded by BMWK · my work since Aug 2024

Sustainable, intelligent building renovation, with partners including Ed. Züblin AG, CAALA, DGNB, STRABAG PFS and Prognos. I built ConSight, an LLM-based system that extracts and analyzes information from building documents to help experts assess a building’s potential for sustainable renovation (WI 2025).

Teaching

Artificial Intelligence in Service Systems (AISS / AISS II)

Master’s courses at KIT · since 2024

I prepare and hold the programming exercises and coach student teams through the semester, including the track Generative AI Applications & Adoption (GAIA).

Thesis supervision

I have supervised 14 bachelor’s and master’s theses, many of them with industry partners such as SAP, Siemens and Netze BW. Topics include:

  • Orchestration and verification of AI agents: designing human handoffs in agentic AI systems
  • Reinforcement learning for retrieval-augmented generation
  • Uncertainty quantification for long-text generation with LLMs
  • LLM-based analysis of public tenders
  • User-centered design of AI-supported document retrieval for digital archives
